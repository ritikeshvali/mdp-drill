The 6 things to name, every time

stateAll info the agent needs to decide, made approximately Markov. Ask: what does the agent see?
actionWhat the agent chooses, not what the environment/user does. Ask: who is the agent, and what can it do?
rewardScalar signal encoding the goal. Watch for reward hacking and short vs long term.
transitionP(s' | s, a). Usually unknown → model-free RL. Say whether it's known.
discount / horizonEpisodic or continuing? What does γ encode here (greedy vs long-term)?
the trapThe one conceptual error that separates a weak answer from a strong one.
